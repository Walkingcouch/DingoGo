# DingoGo Rideshare & Courier: Business Plan Gap Analysis

*Prepared November 2026. Sources are cited inline. Anything I could not confirm against a current primary source is marked **unverified**.*

---

## (A) Critical gaps (fix these before launch)

1. **Uber is about to launch in Alice Springs, and the plan does not cover it.** On 22 Aug 2026 the NT Government welcomed Uber's plan to bring rideshare to Alice Springs "this year". Uber is already recruiting local drivers and already runs Uber Eats in town ([National Tribune](https://www.nationaltribune.com.au/uber-rideshare-on-way-for-alice-springs/); [Alice Springs News, 1 Sep 2026](https://arr.news/2026/09/01/taxis-get-competition/)). Uber and Shebah are both approved Territory-wide dispatch networks. DiDi is not on the list ([NT approved networks](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/communications-and-dispatch-networks/approve-nt-communications-and-dispatch-networks)). DingoGo will be competing with Uber from the start, not with taxis alone. The plan needs a clear differentiation strategy: local ownership, Aboriginal and community trust, pre-booked corporate and NDIS work, courier, pets and tours. It also needs pricing that is not just "cheaper than taxi".
2. **The roof-mounted LED screens could break NT rideshare rules.** Rideshare vehicles "can't carry advertising that would make a reasonable person think they're available for hire" ([NT CPV types](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/types-of-commercial-passenger-vehicles)). A lit roof box looks a lot like a taxi top sign. Get written advice from the NT Passenger Transport Division (08 8924 7580, cpv.compliance@nt.gov.au) before buying screens ([ABLIS](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/nt/ridesharing-licence/40034)). I found no NT rules specific to digital vehicle signage (brightness, motion, video) (**unverified**). Also check ADR and roadworthy rules for roof loads and electrical modifications, and confirm the effect on the Dolphin's warranty and range.
3. **The booking app needs NT approval as a "communications and dispatch network".** Rideshare drivers "can only accept bookings through an approved communications and dispatch network" ([NT CPV types](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/types-of-commercial-passenger-vehicles)). The fee schedule lists *Communication and dispatch network approval $30* and *Operator accreditation $100 (5 years)* ([NT transport industry fees](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/transport-industry-fees)). Build the time for approval into the Spinifex Valley app timeline. The approval criteria are not published online (**unverified**).
4. **Every driver needs full NT accreditation, which is a bottleneck in a small town.** Each driver needs:
   - a current NT open licence, plus 12 months of licence history in the last 3 years
   - to be at least 18.5 years old
   - a national police check and a medical, both dated within 3 months
   - a passed online hire-and-reward knowledge test
   - an H endorsement (valid up to 5 years) and a CPV identity card
   - an ABN, English literacy, and no alcohol interlock condition

   ([NT: become a rideshare driver](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/ridesharing/become-a-rideshare-driver)). Fees: H endorsement $117, theory test $20 ([NT fees](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/transport-industry-fees)). Uber will be recruiting from the same small pool of drivers.
5. **Each vehicle needs a rideshare Commercial Vehicle Licence (CVL).** The CVL costs $300 a year ([NT fees](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/transport-industry-fees); [ABLIS lists $300 for 2026-27](https://ablis.business.gov.au/service/nt/ridesharing-licence/40034)). Each car also needs a roadworthy within 6 weeks of applying, the CVL label on the windscreen, the certificate kept in the car, and the additional MAC insurance premium added to its registration ([NT: apply to use your vehicle](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/ridesharing/apply-to-use-your-vehicle-for-ridesharing)). Sources disagree on maximum vehicle age: NT.gov.au says under 13 years, while ABLIS and the platform information sheet say 9 years. New Dolphins meet both.
6. **The plan does not cost the $1 per-trip levy or its monthly reporting.** Rideshare networks must lodge and pay the $1 passenger services levy every month through an online system. Under-reporting carries criminal penalties ([NT levy](https://nt.gov.au/cpv/levy); [info for rideshare platforms](https://dipl.nt.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0020/446402/information-for-rideshare-platforms.pdf)). The levy is still listed on NT.gov.au, but I could not confirm its 2026 status (**unverified**). Build trip-count reporting into the admin dashboard.
7. **Leased drivers must register for GST before their first trip.** The ATO says ride-sourcing drivers need an ABN and must register for GST "regardless of how much you earn", before their first trip ([ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/sharing-economy-and-tax/ride-sourcing/registrations)). The EV Direct Finance leasing model (~$113.33/week) must include onboarding help with ABN, GST and BAS. The plan should also show what drivers actually earn after GST, lease, insurance and charging.
8. **Tours, Uluru and expansion trips do not fit the Dolphin or the rideshare licence.**
   - **Licensing:** Tours are a separate NT vehicle category. Inside a council area, tour vehicles can operate only as part of a tour package or an approved tour type. They can't do airport transfers outside a package, and they can't price tours by distance ([NT CPV types](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/types-of-commercial-passenger-vehicles)). Check whether a rideshare CVL can legally be used for "private tours", or whether a tour-vehicle licence is also needed. I could not confirm this (**unverified**).
   - **Range:** The Dolphin 60.4 kWh is rated at 427 km WLTP. With 2 kW of air-conditioning that drops to 329 km, and to about 262 km at 120 km/h with A/C ([EVKX](https://evkx.net/models/byd/dolphin/dolphin_60.4kwh/rangeandconsumption)). Ayers Rock Resort is "450km away from the nearest town" ([Ayers Rock Resort](https://www.ayersrockresort.com.au/plan/getting-here)). Alice Springs to Yulara or Tennant Creek cannot be driven without charging on the way, so plan the charging stops. The resort also runs free airport coaches for guests, which weakens the Yulara rideshare case.
9. **The plan has no insurance structure.** The Insurance Council warns that private comprehensive policies generally exclude ride-hail use as "commercial use". Drivers who are not properly insured risk having claims denied ([Insurance Business](https://insurancebusinessmag.com/au/news/breaking-news/ica-urges-nt-ridehail-drivers-to-check-their-motor-vehicle-insurance-105263.aspx)). The NT also requires that drivers are "insured for any injury that occurs while ridesharing" ([NT vehicle page](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/ridesharing/apply-to-use-your-vehicle-for-ridesharing)).

## (C) Recommended app and platform features (prioritised)

**P0 (launch blockers)**
1. **Driver identity and credential vault.** Record H endorsement, CPVID, police check, medical and CVL expiry dates, and suspend drivers automatically when anything expires. Add periodic selfie re-verification.
2. **Rider safety toolkit** to match Uber: in-app emergency button (000 call plus location share), trip sharing with trusted contacts, PIN verification, route-deviation alerts, and optional audio or trip recording ([Uber AU safety](https://www.uber.com/au/en/safety/womens-safety/)). Add an option to request a female driver, as Shebah does in the NT ([NT networks](https://nt.gov.au/driving/industry/communications-and-dispatch-networks/approve-nt-communications-and-dispatch-networks)).
3. **Upfront fixed pricing and itemised receipts with GST.** Receipts must work for ATO and NDIS claims.
4. **Levy and compliance reporting:** monthly trip counts, per-driver GST summaries, and audit logs ([NT levy](https://nt.gov.au/cpv/levy)).
5. **Low-connectivity design:** offline-first driver app, SMS fallback for booking and status, queued trip records, and phone booking through a dispatcher. Outback coverage is patchy, which is why the federal Mobile Black Spot Program is still funding Central Australia sites ([Telstra](https://www.telstra.com.au/aboutus/media/media-releases/new-telstra-mobile-coverage-in-central-australia)). Specific Alice Springs blackspots are **unverified**.
6. **Payment options for unbanked or cash customers:** cash (with driver-safety controls), prepaid vouchers sold through partner shops and community organisations, and account billing for organisations.

**P1 (first 6 months)**
7. Courier module: proof of delivery (photo and signature), prohibited-items checklist (alcohol, weapons), and parcel size and weight limits.
8. Accessibility: screen-reader support, an "assistance animal" flag that drivers cannot refuse, and a wheelchair-accessible vehicle (WAV) request that routes to a WAV partner. The Dolphin is not a WAV.
9. In-app language support: Arrernte and other local languages for key screens, plus audio prompts. Do this with community consultation.
10. Corporate, NDIS and event accounts: invoicing, cost centres, recurring bookings, and plan-manager invoices.
11. EV-aware dispatch: don't assign long trips to cars with low charge, alert when it is time to charge, and adjust range estimates for heat.

**P2**
12. Loyalty programme with café partners. Get consent for any data sharing (see the privacy note in B11).
13. Advertising campaign manager for the screens, once the legality question in A2 is resolved.
14. Scheduled tour bookings with permit checks.

## (D) Business model and revenue additions

- **Revisit the unit economics assumptions.** With only 2–3 cars, 24/7 coverage is not realistic. One car needs about 3 driver shifts a day. Model specific service windows (airport flights, Thursday–Saturday nights, school and medical runs) and a clear utilisation target. The NT Minister cited a need for extra capacity "particularly during busy periods" ([National Tribune](https://www.nationaltribune.com.au/uber-rideshare-on-way-for-alice-springs/)). With only 2–3 cars, DingoGo will lose that peak demand whenever a car is off the road for charging or repairs. Include a backup arrangement (e.g. a partnership with A&P or Wheelies private hire).
- **Pricing benchmark.** Alice Springs taxis charge $5.30 flagfall + $2.45/km by day and $6.60 + $2.89/km at night and on weekends, plus waiting time, a 5% card surcharge and a $50 cleaning fee ([NT taxi, current 22 May 2026](https://nt.gov.au/taxi)). A journalist paid $40.83 for an 8.4 km airport–Connellan trip (~$4.86/km) ([Alice Springs News](https://arr.news/2026/09/01/taxis-get-competition/)). Uber's local price is not yet known.
- **NDIS transport.** Participants receive Transport Allowance of up to $1,606 / $2,472 / $3,456 a year (Levels 1–3) and can pay taxis or rideshare with it, by account invoice to a plan manager or by receipt reimbursement ([Plan Partners](https://planpartners.com.au/knowledge/articles/transport)). Plan-manager invoicing is a ready B2B channel.
- **Government and Aboriginal-organisation contracts:** medical transport (Congress, hospital outpatient), courier services for Aboriginal corporations and land councils, and NT Government work via NTIBN certification. Each of these is worth more than casual rides.
- **Corporate and event accounts:** conferences, the Red CentreNATS and Parrtjima festivals (**unverified** fit), and mining and FIFO transfers.
- **Advertising revenue:** keep it, but consider internal screens or vinyl wraps if roof screens are not allowed (A2).
- **Tours:** consider partnering with existing licensed Aboriginal tour operators rather than running tours under DingoGo's own licence.
- **Courier:** concentrate on same-day local deliveries (pharmacy, legal documents, B2B parts), where national carriers are weak. National competitors (Sendle, CouriersPlease, Australia Post) were not checked against Alice Springs service levels (**unverified**). Local courier listings exist ([Localsearch](https://www.localsearch.com.au/find/couriers/braitling-nt)).
- **Driver leasing:** model what happens if a lessee defaults or leaves, and the residual value of the vehicle after 84 months.

## (F) Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Uber launches first with a larger driver pool and lower fares | Focus on pre-booked, corporate, NDIS, courier and community trust; launch quickly; offer drivers a better deal (vehicle supplied, local support) |
| Not enough accredited drivers | Fund drivers' police checks, medicals and H tests; run community recruitment, including with Aboriginal employment programs |
| Extreme heat: average summer max 36.4°C, often above 40°C ([Alice Springs Town Council](https://alicesprings.nt.gov.au/about-alice-springs/more/climate)) | Expect 20–25% range loss with A/C ([EVKX](https://evkx.net/models/byd/dolphin/dolphin_60.4kwh/rangeandconsumption)); shaded or garaged charging; pre-cooling while plugged in; battery-health monitoring |
| Few public chargers: NRMA DC fast charger opened Mar 2024 ([Katherine Council](https://www.katherine.nt.gov.au/news/fast-charging-ev-stations-ready-to-connect-nt-regions/347)); Tesla 22 kW destination chargers at Crowne Plaza ([Tesla](https://www.tesla.com/findus/location/charger/dc33136)); small AC sites ([Electromaps](https://www.electromaps.com/en/charging-stations/australia/alice-springs-town-council)) | Home charger for each car (~$1,700–$3,000 installed, [EVSE](https://evse.com.au/knowledge-hub/ev-charger-installation-cost)); add solar and battery; NRMA charger kW **unverified** |
| LED screens ruled non-compliant | Get written advice before buying; fall back to vinyl wraps or interior screens |
| Insurance claim denied | Commercial rideshare-endorsed policy; written broker confirmation |
| Alcohol-related incidents or illegal courier carriage | Prohibited-items policy, driver refusal rights, ID checks, no alcohol deliveries |
| Cultural missteps on tours or on Country | CLC permits; Traditional Owner engagement; Respecting Our Culture certification; ICIP protocols ([Tourism NT](https://tourismandeventsnt.com.au/tourism-industry/toolkits-guides/aboriginal-cultural-tourism/recognition-respect)) |
| Fair Work minimum-standards orders extended to rideshare or parcels | Model a $32/hr engaged-time floor for courier work; provide personal accident cover |
| Privacy breach or location-data misuse | Comply with the APPs, minimise and encrypt data, keep a breach response plan |
| Lease default / vehicle residual value | Driver vetting, GPS/immobiliser, insurance on the lease |
